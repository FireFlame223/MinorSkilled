# Frostbrook Town Interiors DLC - Free Sample (Chucky Assets)

19 top-down 16x16 pixel-art tiles and objects from **Frostbrook Town Interiors DLC**: a tavern bar counter (left, taps, right),
an ale barrel, a trestle table and bench, a tavern lantern (3 frames), a mug rack, an anvil, a forge hearth (3 frames),
a quench trough, a goods shelf, flour sacks, a goods crate, a notice board, a chapel pew, a candle stand (3 frames),
a tavern plank floor and a forge stone floor.

Every item comes **with and without shadow**, in **16, 32 and 48 px** (`sprites/<with_shadow|no_shadow>/<size>/<sheet>/`).
Animated objects are separate frames (`_f0`, `_f1`, `_f2`). Same palette and grid as Frostbrook Snow Village.
Works in Godot, Unity, GameMaker, Tiled and RPG Maker.

The full DLC has 243 tiles and objects for a tavern & inn, general store, post office, apothecary, forge, bathhouse and chapel,
with Tiled maps, a Godot 4 TileSet and RPG Maker MZ sheets:
